About NAMMA OORU

A Map-Driven Civic Intelligence Platform for Chennai

NAMMA OORU is an open citizen intelligence platform designed to bridge the gap between Chennai residents and municipal authorities (GCC). By providing high-precision vector road snapping, live device camera capture, and automated GPS geotagging, citizens can report road and infrastructure problems with indisputable authenticity.

Key highlights include:

  • Vector Road Snapping: Automatically associates complaints with specific affected road segments rather than arbitrary pins.
  • In-App Camera Verification: Direct device camera access prevents the upload of reused, downloaded, or duplicate stock photos.
  • Risk-Based Priority: Transparent algorithmic prioritization based on road obstruction, safety risk, and report age.
